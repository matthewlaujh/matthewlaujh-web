Reconstructed Colours2026

A suspended line of light that reconstructs colour from a latent space.

Exhibited at ‘Techno Terra: Archive of the Future’ at Gallery 1, Institute of Contemporary Arts Singapore, LASALLE College of the Arts (4 Sep – 24 Oct 2026)

Part of a series of work exploring Machine Learning and Light — other works in series Ambi(latent) Space (2025), Deconstructed Ambiance (2025) and Deconstructed Colours (2023).

Reconstructed Colours (2026) is a suspended generative light object that returns to the latent space of Deconstructed Colours and rebuilds it. A bare strip of light hangs from the ceiling on its own wiring, its colour field reassembled from the same machine-learning principles — high and low dimensional spaces, diffusion and algorithmic plots — this time read back into the room as a single line of light and the glow it leaves on the floor.

Where the earlier work took colour apart to make a latent space tangible, this one puts it back together: the fixture continuously reconstructs palettes from its inputs, so the colour of the room is never fixed, only recomposed.
