Circulated Densities2025

A circuit board that shows Singapore’s mobility as live data.

Exhibited at ‘Singapore: A Design Thesis in the Making’ at Gallery 1, Institute of Contemporary Arts Singapore (12 September–28 October 2025)

Circulated Densities (2025) is a responsive circuit topology visualising the city’s mobility infrastructure through real-time data. A Printed Circuit Board materialises Singapore’s systematic approach to urban density management, where algorithmic planning orchestrates human circulation. This living diagram interrogates how designed systems mediate between individual agency and collective movement in hyper-dense urban space.

Drawing from Singapore’s moniker as “The Little Red Dot,” the piece takes the form of a circle — an abstracted visualisation of the city’s shape while remaining rooted in urban planning masterplans with LEDs illuminating each of the 332 subzones. The intensity of light represents the density of each area at a given time through representative data collected from public transit and carpark availability open data sources.
