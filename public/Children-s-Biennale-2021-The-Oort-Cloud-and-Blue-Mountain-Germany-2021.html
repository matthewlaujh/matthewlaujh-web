Children's Biennale 2021: The Oort Cloud and Blue Mountain, Germany2021

Vinyl adaptation of the artists’ work for the Children’s Biennale, Dresden.

Completed while with Neuewave for Andreas Schlegel & Hazel Lim-Schlegel. Photo credit: SKD Museum

The Oort Cloud and the Blue Mountain for The Children’s Biennale 2021 in Germany is an installation work that refers both to Blue Mountain, an early 20th century painting by Wassily Kandinsky and the Oort Cloud, an astronomical phenomenon described as an extended shell of icy objects that exist on the outer reaches of our solar system.

Adapted artists' original artwork into vinyl sticker designs.
