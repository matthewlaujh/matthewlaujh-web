Hey, What’s Up?2022

An interactive installation that visualises the spiral of our thoughts.

ExperientialR/GA (internship)

Scroll↓

Year
2022
Category
Experiential
Techniques
Generative AI, speech input, projection
Client
R/GA (internship)

Completed as part of an internship project with R/GA and as a personal practice investigating the way we think and how generative AI plays a role in that.

Hey, What’s Up? (2022) is an interactive installation designed to visualise what goes on in our mind, representing the layers of thought as we spiral outwards. Audiences are invited to input their thoughts through a microphone. The installation helps us express our thoughts and process them by probing users with open-ended prompts with every interaction.

The internal conversation will then be externalised, logged and printed onto a looping piece of paper. Articulated thoughts are intentionally printed on top of each other, showing the overlapping nature of how thoughts manifest naturally.
