The Oort Cloud and the Blue Mountain2021–2022

Vinyl adaptation and LED electronics for an installation after Kandinsky, across three venues.

ExperientialAndreas Schlegel & Hazel Lim-Schlegel, with Neuewave/MDRN

Year
2021–2022
Category

Techniques

Client
Andreas Schlegel & Hazel Lim-Schlegel, with Neuewave/MDRN

Completed while with Neuewave/MDRN for Andreas Schlegel & Hazel Lim-Schlegel. Shown at the Children’s Biennale 2021, Dresden (photo: SKD Museum), the Kinderbiënnale 2021, Groningen (photo: Groninger Museum), and Singapore Art Week 2022 at Singapore Art Museum, Tanjong Pagar Distripark.

The Oort Cloud and the Blue Mountain is an installation work by Andreas Schlegel and Hazel Lim-Schlegel that refers both to Blue Mountain, an early twentieth-century painting by Wassily Kandinsky, and to the Oort Cloud, the extended shell of icy objects at the outer reaches of our solar system.

For the two biennale showings I adapted the artists’ original artwork into vinyl designs for the gallery walls. For Singapore Art Week I did the same and also developed the electronic components and setup for the LED light installations.
