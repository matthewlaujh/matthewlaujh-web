Completed as part of the BA in Design Communication Level 3 programme at LASALLE.

A Series of Explorations and Technical Challenges is an archive of challenges I set myself to learn the technical skills behind physical computing.

It records each step taken to solve them, through exploration and iteration, and was built to support my dissertation and graduation project. Each exploration includes video and photo documentation of the circuit and the circuit diagram and code used to program it.

It was written as a guide, for me and for others, to simple interactions across a range of inputs and outputs. It has been maintained since and used by other students as a start in physical prototyping.
