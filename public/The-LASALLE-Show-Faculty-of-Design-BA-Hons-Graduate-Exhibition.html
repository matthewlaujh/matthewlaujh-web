The LASALLE Show: Faculty of Design BA (Hons) Graduate Exhibition Frontage2022

An LED frontage for the Faculty of Design graduate exhibition.

Exhibited as part of The LASALLE Show at the Institute
of Contemporary Arts Singapore (20 May–1 June 2022)

Conceptualized, fabricated and installed an LED Installation that serves as the first point of interest for visitors to the Faculty of Design BA (Hons) Graduate Exhibition at The LASALLE Show Exhibition 2022.

The show’s theme was ‘proximate’ — the return to tangibility, real and perceived, between people and objects.

The installation was built to draw viewers in, where the text only reveals itself when light is being passed behind it. The lights were programmed to fade at different paces, creating tension between each passing motion revealing parts of the text slowly as viewers take their time to read through it.
