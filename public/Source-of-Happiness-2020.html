Source of Happiness2020

A data study of how experts measure happiness, with a sound visualiser.

Happiness is a subjective term. It can mean a feeling or a mood, and it is perceived differently from person to person, so I studied how experts define the factors in their rankings of happiness, then looked at the macro level — how different regions rank — and compiled a second data set from the original source.

Music is a source of happiness for many, so I built an audio visualiser that draws a graphic as a song plays, to capture happiness through sound. The graphics interpret music as sine waves and borrow from the grooves pressed into records.

Using data from the World Happiness Report 2020, the p5.js sketch is framed as an app interface so an overview of the data is easy to reach — at first glance the raw report is overwhelming.
