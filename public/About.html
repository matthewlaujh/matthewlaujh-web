About me

I am a creative technologist and artist working at the intersection of computational design, emerging technology and light. My experience spans building AI-driven tools and interactive media for global brands, at Accenture Song and through my studio Organised Mess, to creating installations that translate computational systems into human-centred experiences. In my own practice, shown in Singapore and New York, I use machine learning and custom hardware to make the processes inside a system visible as light in a room.

Whether prototyping for clients or developing my own practice, I combine technical experimentation with design-led thinking to deliver outcomes that are both innovative and tangible.

—

Artist statement

My work explores the relationship between people and machines through interactive light. Light is a medium that connects across different kinds of understanding, and through responsive lighting environments I turn abstract computational ideas into sensory experiences that can be felt before they are explained.

By making visible what usually stays hidden — the algorithms, processes and machine-learning principles that increasingly shape our world — the work pushes back on the idea of artificial intelligence as an inscrutable black box. Earlier pieces made latent spaces legible as colour and light. The newer ones ask what such a system does when it is left alone with itself, learning from its own output until its range collapses.

Underneath this is a belief that technology should deepen human connection rather than thin it out. The installations treat the machine as a collaborator rather than a tool, setting up a dialogue in which human intention and computational process both shape what is finally seen.

—
