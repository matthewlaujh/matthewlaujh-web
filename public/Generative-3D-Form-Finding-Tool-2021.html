Generative 3D Form Finding Tool2021

A tool that grows 3D forms from supershape equations.

Completed as part of a self-initiated research tool with Neuewave.

This Generative 3D Form Finding Tool (2021) was developed based on the concept of supershapes which are an extension of the equations of the sphere, ellipse and superellipse. By manipulating the values in the mathematical formula, rotational symmetry, polygonal shapes, asymmetrical forms and spherical shapes can be achieved.
